Split Black Moong Dal (Moong Dal Chilka)

Home-style split moong dal with skin, finished with a garlic-cumin ghee tadka. Serves 3–4.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup split moong dal with skin (moong dal chilka)
  • 3 cups water
  • 1/2 tsp turmeric powder
  • 3/4 to 1 tsp salt, or to taste
  • 1 medium tomato, chopped
  • 1 to 2 green chillies, slit
  • 1/2 tsp grated ginger
  • 1 to 1 1/2 tbsp ghee
  • 1 tsp cumin seeds
  • 1 pinch hing
  • 2 to 3 garlic cloves, sliced
  • 1 dried red chilli
  • 1/2 tsp red chilli powder
  • Fresh coriander, chopped
  • 1/2 tsp garam masala (optional)
  • 1/2 tsp kasuri methi (optional)
  • 1 small knob butter (optional)

Directions

  1. Wash the dal thoroughly and soak for 30–60 minutes. Drain.
  2. Pressure cook with 3 cups water, turmeric, tomato, ginger and green chilli for about 3 whistles. Let pressure release naturally.
  3. Stir, add salt and extra hot water if needed, then simmer for 3–5 minutes.
  4. Heat ghee for tadka. Add cumin, hing, garlic and dried red chilli. Fry until garlic is lightly golden.
  5. Lower or switch off heat, add red chilli powder and immediately pour over the dal.
  6. Finish with coriander and optional garam masala. For dhaba style, add kasuri methi and butter.
