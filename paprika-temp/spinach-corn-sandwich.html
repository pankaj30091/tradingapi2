Healthy Spinach & Corn Grilled Sandwich

A healthy grilled spinach and sweet corn sandwich using skyr instead of mayonnaise or cheese. Makes 3 sandwiches.

Ingredients

  • 1½ cups spinach, finely chopped
  • ¾ cup sweet corn, boiled
  • 3–5 tbsp skyr
  • ½ small onion, finely chopped
  • 6 slices whole-wheat or multigrain bread
  • ½ tsp roasted cumin powder
  • ½ tsp black pepper
  • Chilli flakes or finely chopped green chilli, to taste
  • Salt, to taste
  • ½ tsp chaat masala (optional)
  • 1 tsp olive oil or butter (optional)
  • 100–150 g crumbled paneer (optional, for extra protein)
  • A little chopped garlic (optional)

Directions

  1. Heat a pan and cook the chopped spinach for 2–3 minutes until wilted and most of its water has evaporated. Optional: sauté a little garlic with the spinach. Let cool.
  2. Roughly crush about half the boiled corn with a fork, leaving the remainder whole for texture.
  3. Mix the cooled spinach, corn and onion. Start with 3 tbsp skyr.
  4. Add cumin, pepper, chilli, salt and optional chaat masala. Add more skyr only as needed; the mixture should be creamy but not wet.
  5. For a higher-protein version, mix in 100–150 g crumbled paneer.
  6. Divide the filling between three pairs of bread slices.
  7. Grill until crisp and golden. Grill dry or lightly brush the outside with olive oil or butter.

Tip: Keep the spinach and filling fairly dry to prevent the grilled sandwich becoming soggy.
