Healthy Skyr Paneer Sandwich

A high-protein paneer sandwich with skyr, onion and tomato. Makes 3 regular sandwiches from 200 g paneer.

Ingredients

  • 200 g paneer, crumbled or grated
  • 1 small onion, finely chopped
  • 1 medium tomato, deseeded and finely chopped
  • 3–4 tbsp skyr curd
  • 1/2 tsp roasted cumin powder
  • 1/2 tsp black pepper
  • Salt to taste
  • Chilli flakes or finely chopped green chilli, to taste (optional)
  • 6 slices whole-wheat or multigrain bread
  • A very light brushing of olive oil or butter for toasting (optional)

Directions

  1. Combine the paneer, onion, tomato and skyr in a bowl.
  2. Season with roasted cumin powder, black pepper, salt and optional chilli. Mix gently.
  3. If the filling becomes watery, drain off any excess liquid before assembling.
  4. Divide the filling equally between three sandwiches.
  5. Toast in a sandwich maker or on a pan over medium heat until crisp and warmed through. Toast dry, or use only a very light brushing of oil or butter.

Storage

Store unused filling in an airtight container in the refrigerator. Best used within 24 hours; if kept continuously at 4°C or below, use within 48 hours. Stir before using and drain any separated liquid.

Tip: If making the filling in advance, keep the chopped tomato separate and add it just before making the sandwich to reduce wateriness.
